Join Us

Our team is driven by a single mission: To advance the efficiency frontier of robust & safe physical AI through fully open and reproducible research. If you are excited about physical AI, world models and autonomy, and want to join us on this mission, apply for one of the following roles:

Technical Staff: Research Scientist

Lead original research that advances robust and safe physical AI. We are looking for someone with a strong, published research track record in a domain relevant to KE:SAI's mission who can develop a focused research agenda and publish reproducible work with open models and code. You will have the opportunity to mentor interns and PhD students. This is a permanent position.

Technical Staff: Research Engineer

Turn promising research ideas into stable, efficient, large-scale training runs. We are looking for someone with deep experience in distributed computing who understands the full training stack, from simulation and model code down to GPU kernels. You should be able to turn careful profiling into practical improvements and diagnose difficult performance or stability failures. This is a permanent position.

Technical Staff: Infrastructure Engineer

Build and operate the infrastructure that makes ambitious physical AI research possible. We are looking for someone with experience operating large-scale training platforms who can help maintain and grow our compute cluster that combines simulation- and training-specialized GPUs. Working closely with researchers, you will translate their needs into infrastructure that makes large-scale experimentation more efficient. This is a permanent position.

PhD Student

Pursue a PhD at the cutting edge of world models or physical AI, supervised by world-leading researchers. We are looking for someone curious, with a strong academic foundation in a relevant technical field, who wants to develop their own ideas in a collaborative, well-resourced environment. You will pursue open long-term research problems in world models or physical AI and publish impactful and fully reproducible research. Please note that our PhD hiring is conducted through the ELLIS PhD program's industry track.

Your Profile

You have great analytical skills, are highly-motivated, curious, creative, and a good team player. You have a very good mathematical understanding, great coding skills, and prior research or engineering experience related to KE:SAI's mission. If you apply for a Technical Staff position, you have earned a PhD and authored papers at top-tier conferences in computer vision, robotics, or machine learning, or have equivalent industry experience. You are excited about KE:SAI's mission, committed to open science, and are looking to work with some of the most talented researchers in the field at the frontier of AI. Is this you? Then join us on our mission!

Your Application

To apply for one of the technical staff positions, send your application package as one single pdf with maximally 10 pages to: talent@kesai.eu. Your application ideally includes the following information in this order:

  • A Cover Letter in which you state (i) why you want to work for KE:SAI and (ii) your most significant accomplishment. We are particularly interested in the how: the steps you took, and what you learned.
  • Your Curriculum Vitae (CV) including links to your Google Scholar, Github, and a list of reference letter writers that know you and can be contacted by us.
  • Your Research Statement describing your prior research as well as your future agenda. Include links to research papers or technical documentation.
  • Your University Transcripts, for both undergraduate as well as graduate level studies (if applicable).

Our PhD hiring is conducted through the ELLIS PhD program's industry track. If you are interested in this position, please apply to the next ELLIS PhD program cycle, selecting Andreas Geiger, Kashyap Chitta, or Bernhard Schölkopf as advisors.
